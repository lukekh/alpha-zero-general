How to play

Move one square in any direction. Reach the opposite corner to win. A player with no legal moves loses.

✊ Rock captures scissors
✌️ Scissors captures paper
✋ Paper captures rock

Friendly pieces and enemies you cannot capture block their squares. Captures are optional.

Repetition and turns without captures do not end a played game. The AI uses draw limits internally when modelling possible continuations.
