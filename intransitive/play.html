How to play

Move one square in any direction. Reach the opposite corner to win. A player with no legal moves loses.

R Rock captures scissors
S Scissors captures paper
P Paper captures rock

Friendly pieces and enemies you cannot capture block their squares. Captures are optional.

Training draw rules are active

This engine also draws on threefold repetition or 30 total turns without a capture. These are modelling limits, not official game rules. A win takes precedence.
